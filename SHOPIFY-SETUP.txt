NUTS FOR CHRIST STOREFRONT

The storefront is implemented as Shopify Online Store 2.0 Liquid sections.
Push this checkout to the GitHub branch connected to your draft theme. Nothing
has been pushed or published by this implementation.

AFTER THE GITHUB SYNC

1. Open Online Store > Themes > your draft theme > Customize.
2. Open Theme settings > NFC storefront for the new colors, fonts, page width,
   cart labels, and shopping messages. The older theme settings remain available
   for the retained legacy sections; the new storefront uses NFC settings.
3. In the Header group, choose your navigation menu and logo, and edit the
   announcement, wordmark, and tagline. Without a selected menu, the header
   links to Home, Shop all, /pages/contact, and /pages/faq.
4. On the home page, edit and reorder the NFC sections. Replace campaign images
   using the image pickers. Each collection tile supports a collection picker,
   image override, title, caption, and link override. Set the hoodie link in the
   hero to the correct collection. Until you select collections, collection
   links and featured products use Shopify's all-products collection.
5. Select the featured collection and product count. Products, prices, images,
   discounts, variants, availability, and descriptions come from Shopify.
6. Create or update your Contact and FAQ pages in Shopify. Use the handles
   contact and faq for the default navigation, or choose your own menu links.
   Assign the contact template to Contact and the faq (or faqs) template to FAQ.
   Edit FAQ questions and answers as blocks in the theme editor. The included
   answers are neutral starting points; confirm your actual store policies.
7. Enter your actual policies in Shopify Settings > Policies. The footer
   automatically links to published policies. You can override the Information
   menu in the Footer group. Policy content is managed in Shopify admin; its
   presentation follows the new storefront. Optional page templates named
   shipping-policy, returns-policy, privacy, and terms can display normal page
   content if you prefer separate information pages.
8. Edit the footer menus, copy, newsletter heading, social-link blocks, and
   payment-icon visibility. Newsletter forms submit to Shopify's customer form;
   contact messages submit through Shopify's contact form. Confirm your store
   contact email and marketing settings, and test submissions in your preview.
9. Configure collection filters in Shopify Search & Discovery. The catalog
   displays Shopify's configured filters, sorting, and pagination. Filtering
   submits to Shopify and works without JavaScript.
10. Product settings control vendor display, availability copy, accelerated
    checkout, related collection, and the optional save button. Product detail
    accordions support editable rich text or a Shopify page. App blocks are
    supported in the product section. Create a page using the wishlist template
    and handle wishlist if you use Saved pieces. Favorites stay on that browser
    and device; they do not synchronize with customer accounts.

TEMPLATES AND CONTENT

Default and existing alternate product, collection, blog, and standard content
page templates now use the new NFC sections. This also covers resources already
assigned one of the old demo template names. The wishlist template uses saved
pieces; contact and FAQ templates have their specialized sections. Existing
Shopify product/page content is read dynamically. Custom content formerly saved
only in old demo template sections is replaced by the new layout; it remains
recoverable from Git history. Add any required content to the resource in Shopify
or to an editable NFC section. Original legacy section and asset files remain in
the repository, but their old global scripts/styles are not loaded by the new
storefront layout.

Search, collection listings, cart, blog, articles, 404, and classic customer
account templates have matching designs. Shopify's new customer accounts and
checkout are hosted by Shopify and use Shopify's own branding controls. The
existing password and issued gift-card templates retain their original behavior.

BEFORE PUBLISHING

Preview your actual products and variants, inventory states, store currency,
filters, discounts, cart quantities, checkout, subscription/app integrations if
used, contact and newsletter submissions, and the theme editor on mobile and
desktop. Selling plans use Shopify data, but their app-specific pricing and
purchase flows should be verified with your actual catalog. Products and pages
must be published to the Online Store sales channel to appear.

LOCAL VERIFICATION

Shopify Theme Check reported no issues in the new NFC Liquid files, new layout,
or global settings schema. The original checkout had 98 Theme Check errors in
legacy files; these remain and are not introduced by the new storefront.

Local browser checks used a LiquidJS preview with sample products and mocked
Shopify cart responses. Verified variant/price/URL changes, unavailable variants,
quick view, add to cart, quantity changes, removal, mobile navigation, keyboard
dismissal, FAQ expansion, and overflow at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. These checks
do not replace rendering and transaction checks in a real Shopify draft theme.
